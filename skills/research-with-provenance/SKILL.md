# research-with-provenance

Use when current vendor/tool/library/standard evidence materially affects a decision.

Prefer primary official documentation and dated engineering reports. Record source date/version when freshness matters.

Classify claims:
- `KNOWN`
- `INFERRED`
- `UNKNOWN`

## Mutation authority
Research is read-only unless the task separately authorizes adoption.

## Output
Source-aware research packet, validity boundary, adoption implications.
